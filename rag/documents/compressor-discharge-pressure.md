# Compressor 204 Discharge Pressure Troubleshooting Guide

## 1. Scope and symptoms

This guide supports the first response to high discharge pressure on
Compressor 204 (COMP-204). Alarm ALM-1002 is high severity. Confirm the
transmitter value against the local indication and review the pressure trend
before changing the operating state.

## 2. Immediate response

1. Acknowledge ALM-1002 and record the time and operating load.
2. Inspect the discharge strainer differential and verify valve travel.
3. Check condensate drains and confirm that the downstream path is available.
4. Reduce loading if the compressor approaches its trip limit.
5. Notify the supervisor if pressure remains high after the first checks.

Do not bypass trips or open pressurized equipment. Follow the safe-isolation
instruction before inspection or maintenance.

## 3. Troubleshooting

### 3.1 Discharge strainer restriction

Compare the differential pressure with the approved limit and inspect trend
history. A steadily increasing differential suggests restriction. Schedule
cleaning through the maintenance process; do not remove the strainer cover
while pressurized.

### 3.2 Condensate accumulation

Verify that automatic and manual drains are available and that the drain
route is safe. Unexpected liquid carryover requires supervisor review before
continuing operation.

### 3.3 Valve or downstream restriction

Compare commanded and actual valve positions. Check related equipment and
process demand. If the pressure continues to rise, reduce load and prepare
for a controlled shutdown in accordance with the operating envelope.

## 4. Escalation

Escalate when pressure approaches the trip limit, unusual vibration or noise
is observed, the local and remote indications disagree, or the compressor
cannot maintain stable operation. Preserve trends and alarm history for
handover.

# Boiler Feed Pump 102 Operating Procedure

## 1. Purpose and scope

This procedure guides control-room operators responding to abnormal
conditions on Boiler Feed Pump 102 (BFP-102) at EastRefinery. It applies to
normal operation, alarm response, controlled load reduction, and preparation
for maintenance. Follow the approved site isolation procedure before any
hands-on work.

## 2. Alarm condition

Alarm ALM-1001 indicates high discharge pressure. The alarm is critical when
pressure continues to rise, the pump approaches its trip threshold, or the
local pressure gauge disagrees with the transmitter. Repeated occurrences
should be reported because they may indicate a recurring valve, strainer, or
downstream restriction.

## 3. Immediate operator response

1. Acknowledge the alarm and confirm the indication against the local gauge.
2. Verify that the discharge valve is in the expected operating position.
3. Check suction pressure and the suction strainer differential.
4. Confirm that the downstream process path is available and not blocked.
5. Monitor pressure trend, pump vibration, and motor current.
6. If pressure continues to increase, reduce loading according to the
   operating envelope and notify the control-room supervisor.

Do not open, loosen, or drain a live process connection. Do not repeatedly
reset the alarm without identifying the cause.

## 4. Likely causes and checks

### 4.1 Partially closed discharge valve

Compare the commanded valve position with the field position indication.
Check for a travel or actuator fault. If the valve cannot be placed in the
approved position, maintain a safe operating state and escalate to operations
and maintenance.

### 4.2 Suction strainer fouling

Compare suction pressure and strainer differential with the approved
operating limit. A rising differential can indicate fouling or restricted
flow. Do not remove the strainer cover while the pump or connected line is
pressurized.

### 4.3 Downstream restriction

Check downstream valve availability and process demand. Compare the pressure
trend with other pumps and related equipment. If the restriction cannot be
identified safely, reduce load and request engineering support.

## 5. Escalation and trip criteria

Escalate immediately when pressure exceeds the trip threshold, vibration
increases, the local gauge and transmitter disagree, motor current is
abnormal, or the pump loses stable flow. If the pump trips, preserve the
alarm and trend information for the shift handover.

## 6. Safe isolation and restart

Maintenance requires depressurization, isolation of process and electrical
energy, lockout/tagout, and confirmation of zero energy. Only authorized
personnel may perform the isolation. After maintenance, inspect the work
area, remove tools, verify guards, and complete the approved pre-start check
before returning BFP-102 to service.

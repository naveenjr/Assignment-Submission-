# Safe Isolation and Lockout/Tagout Instruction

## 1. Purpose

This instruction defines the minimum control-room handover and field
verification steps before opening process equipment, electrical cabinets, or
instrument connections. It applies to BFP-102, COMP-204, MTR-305, and
equivalent equipment.

## 2. Preparation

Review the work scope, equipment identity, current alarms, process hazards,
and approved work permit. Confirm the isolation boundary with the responsible
operations authority. Identify process pressure, electrical supply, stored
mechanical energy, hot surfaces, and any connected or backflow sources.

## 3. Isolation sequence

1. Stop or place the equipment in the approved safe state.
2. Close and secure the required process isolation valves.
3. Isolate electrical, hydraulic, pneumatic, and mechanical energy sources.
4. Apply personal and group locks according to the site lockout/tagout rule.
5. Drain, vent, discharge, or restrain stored energy using approved points.
6. Test for zero energy with an approved method.
7. Record the isolation and obtain authorization before work begins.

Never rely on a control-system command alone as proof of isolation. Never
open a process connection until pressure and stored energy have been
verified as zero.

## 4. Return to service

Confirm that tools and personnel are clear, guards are installed, drains and
vents are restored, and all parties release their locks through the approved
process. Notify the control room, complete the pre-start checklist, and
monitor the equipment for abnormal pressure, temperature, vibration, or
current after restart.

## 5. Stop-work conditions

Stop work and contact the responsible authority if the equipment identity is
uncertain, an isolation point cannot be secured, pressure does not decay as
expected, a lock is missing, or the work scope changes.
